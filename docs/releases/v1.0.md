## What it does

A bubble sits over the text field you are typing in. You tap the bubble,
talk, tap again, and your words land at the cursor.

An accessibility service watches for focused text fields and tells the
bubble when to show. The bubble lives in a foreground service with an
overlay window.

## Dictation

Transcription runs on the phone. Audio stays on the device.

Two engines ship in the app. A settings pick chooses one, and the app
falls back to the other while the picked engine's model is still
downloading:

- Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, the default. It covers 25 European languages.
  The model download is about 640 MiB.
- Whisper, the base.en model from whisper.cpp. It covers 99 languages
  and serves as the fallback.

On device, the transcript runs through the baseline post-processor, your
personal dictionary, and casing rules before anything else touches it.

## Styles

Pick a style and your dictation gets rewritten in that voice on the way
in. Facts, names, numbers, dates, and quoted text stay as dictated in
every style. Style changes the telling.

- Two mechanical styles run on the phone as plain string transforms.
  They cost nothing and need no account.
- 32 cloud styles run through a Firebase Function that calls a language
  model. They sit in six sections: Serious, Ancient Philosophers,
  Writers, Eras and Genres, Personas, and Modern Parody.
- Custom styles are yours: describe a voice in the app and the server
  generates the style definition.

If styling fails or the subscription check says no, the app tells you
and the raw transcript stays yours to use. Dictation itself never waits
on the network.

## Accounts and the subscription

On-device dictation works signed out. It is free and carries no usage
limit.

Cloud styles need a Google sign-in and a subscription: $2.99 per month
with a one-week free trial, bought through Google Play. Cloud styling
carries a monthly fair-use cap counted in normalized tokens. One
dictation can run as long as you keep talking; the cap counts the
month's total volume.
